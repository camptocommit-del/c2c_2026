2026 Draft Lottery

A draft order nobody can steer, and that you can build yourself in about ten seconds.

How this works

The teams are locked into this page. That list of twelve has a fingerprint — the long code down in the next paragraph. Change even one letter of one team name and that code comes out completely different, so the code posted in the league chat is proof the roster hasn't been touched since.

Nobody rolls the dice. The order gets built from a number nobody in the league controls: where the S&P 500 closes on Tuesday, September 1. Feed a number into this page and it works like a random number generator — out comes a scrambled order that looks random but lands exactly the same way every time for that same number.

Try it right now. Type any number you feel like into the box below and press the button. You'll get a random-looking board. Type that same number again and you'll get that same board back. Change one digit and the whole thing reshuffles. That's the whole trick. Nobody can aim it, because Tuesday's close doesn't exist yet. And nobody can fake it, because once it does exist you can type it in here yourself and see what comes out.

Which is the short version of why the first change below exists.


    
What changed for 2026

    
Scoring is untouched. Every value carried over from last season — passing, rushing, receiving, kicking and the defense tiers are all identical. The changes are structural.


    

      
You play the league median every week

      
2025: off → 2026: on

      
On top of your head-to-head matchup, your score gets compared to the middle score in the league that week. So each week produces two results and you can go 2–0, 1–1 or 0–2. Put up the third-highest score and run into the second-highest, and you still bank a win instead of eating a loss. Over fourteen weeks it takes a lot of the schedule luck out of who makes the six.

    


    

      
Two more bench spots

      
5 bench → 7 bench

      
Rosters go from 15 to 17. Starting lineups don't change at all. With twelve teams that's 24 more players held league-wide, so the waiver wire will run thinner than you're used to — handcuffs and stashes get more expensive to chase.

    


    

      
Two IR slots

      
none → 2

      
You can park two injured players without burning bench spots. Only players ruled Out or Suspended qualify — Doubtful and Questionable don't, so you can't hide a healthy scratch there.

    


    

      
Bench players lock at kickoff

      
2025: off → 2026: on

      
A bench player is frozen once his game starts, same as a starter. No more sliding someone into your lineup after you've already seen him play.
